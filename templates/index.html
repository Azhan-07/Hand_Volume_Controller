HandVolumizer 🖐️

Control audio volume with hand gestures, using nothing but your webcam. Pinch your thumb and index finger to set the volume of the built-in tone from 0–100%. All tracking runs locally in your browser.

Gesture guide

👊 Fist (hold ~0.6s)Toggle between IDLE and VOLUME mode
✋ Flat handActive volume posture
🤏 Pinch (thumb + index)Set volume 0–100% (VOLUME mode only)
✌️ V signMute / unmute

Web edition of the HandVolumizer repo. Browsers can't change your system volume, so the gestures control the volume of the built-in tone instead (the desktop app in hand.py controls your real system volume with pycaw).
